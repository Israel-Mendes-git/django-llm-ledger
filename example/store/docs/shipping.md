# Shipping

## Where we ship
We ship to every state in Brazil and to Portugal, the United States and Canada. We do not ship to other countries yet.

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 2 pm (Fortaleza time) on business days leave our roastery the same day. Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days in Ceará, 4 to 8 business days elsewhere in Brazil, and 10 to 20 business days internationally.

## Shipping costs
Shipping is free in Brazil for orders above R$ 150. Below that, shipping costs R$ 19.90 flat. International shipping is calculated at checkout and customs fees are paid by the customer.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking code by email as soon as the package leaves the roastery.
