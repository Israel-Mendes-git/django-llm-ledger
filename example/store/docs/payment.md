# Payment

## Accepted methods
We accept Pix, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Elo, Amex) and boleto. International customers can pay with credit card or PayPal.

## Installments
Credit card purchases above R$ 200 can be split into up to 3 installments without interest.

## Pix discount
Paying with Pix gives a 5% discount, applied automatically at checkout.

## Boleto
Boleto orders are reserved for 3 days. The order ships after the payment clears, which takes up to 2 business days.
