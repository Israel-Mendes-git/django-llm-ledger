# Contact and warranty

## Talking to our team
To talk to a person, email help@serraverde.example or send a WhatsApp message to +55 85 0000-0000. You can also use the "Talk to a human" button in the chat and we will email you back.

## Support hours
Our team is open and answers Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm, and Saturday, 8 am to noon (Fortaleza time). Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.

## Visiting the roastery
The roastery in Guaramiranga is open for visits and tastings on Saturdays from 9 am to 1 pm. Booking is required by email.

## Equipment warranty
Grinders and brewers have a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Damage from drops or misuse is not covered.

## Wholesale
Cafés and restaurants can request wholesale prices by writing to wholesale@serraverde.example.
