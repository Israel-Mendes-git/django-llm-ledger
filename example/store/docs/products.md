# Our coffee

## Origin
All our coffee is arabica grown in the Maciço de Baturité mountains in Ceará, between 800 and 1,100 meters of altitude, by family farms we buy from directly.

## Roasts
We roast three profiles: light (fruity, for filter), medium (chocolate and caramel, our best seller) and dark (intense, for espresso). Every bag shows the roast date; we ship within 7 days of roasting.

## Bag sizes and grind
Bags come in 250 g and 1 kg. You can choose whole beans or ground for espresso, moka pot, French press or paper filter.

## Storage
Keep the bag closed with the valve facing up, away from light and heat. Whole beans stay at their best for about 6 weeks after roasting; ground coffee for about 2 weeks. Do not store coffee in the fridge.
