# Coffee subscription

## How it works
The subscription delivers fresh coffee every 2 or 4 weeks. You choose the roast, grind and bag size, and get 10% off every delivery plus free shipping in Brazil.

## Changing or pausing
You can change the roast, skip a delivery or pause the subscription at any time from your account page, up to 2 days before the next shipment.

## Cancelling
There is no minimum period and no cancellation fee. Cancel from your account page or by writing to help@serraverde.example.
